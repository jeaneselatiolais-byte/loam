Key Metrics to Track

Measure what matters at each phase.

Awareness Metrics

• Impressions & reach per platform • Follower growth rate • Video view count & completion rate • Hashtag usage (#Habitra, #HabitraChallenge) • Share/save rate on TikTok

Conversion Metrics

• Cost per install (CPI) by platform • Click-through rate (CTR) on ads • App Store page conversion rate • Free → Pro conversion rate • Day 7 / Day 30 retention

Engagement Metrics

• Engagement rate by post type • Comment sentiment • UGC submissions (#HabitraChallenge) • Community growth (shares, saves) • Reply/DM volume

Revenue Metrics

• Revenue per install • LTV by acquisition channel • ROAS (return on ad spend) • Pro trial start rate • Subscription retention (month 2+)
