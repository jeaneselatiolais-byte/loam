Production Notes

1. Apple Requirements: App previews must be 15-30 seconds, recorded from actual device or simulator footage. Use iPhone 15 Pro Max (1290x2796) for 6.7" display class. H.264 codec, 30fps.
2. Recording: Use Xcode Simulator screen recording (Cmd+R) or QuickTime Player mirroring a physical device. The HTML screenshot mockups in screenshots.html can serve as reference frames.
3. Audio: Background music must be royalty-free. Keep it minimal and upbeat. Sound effects for completions and celebrations add polish. Apple allows audio in previews.
4. Text Overlays: Keep overlaid text large (44pt+) and brief. Use Habitra's brand font (Inter or SF Pro). Ensure contrast meets accessibility standards.
5. Pacing: Quick cuts keep energy high. Each scene should have clear motion - scrolling, tapping, animations filling. Avoid static frames longer than 2 seconds.
6. End Cards: Apple requires the last frame to be meaningful. Show the app logo, a clear tagline, and optionally the App Store badge. Hold for 2 seconds minimum.
7. Order in App Store: Video 1 (Daily Flow) as primary, Video 3 (Streak Journey) as second, Video 2 (AI Features) as third. Lead with the core experience.
