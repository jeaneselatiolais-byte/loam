## 3 Short Video Clip Concepts (15-30 seconds each)

### Video 1 — "A Day with Habitra" (15 sec)
**Concept:** Quick montage of a user's daily flow
- Morning: iPhone wakes up, Lock Screen shows Habitra streak widget
- Tap: Open app, see Today tab with AI coaching message "Good morning! 3 habits scheduled."
- Complete: Tap "Meditate" circle — pulse animation, streak badge increments to 14
- Mood: Quick mood check-in tap (happy face)
- End: Progress ring fills to 100%, confetti burst
- **End card:** Habitra logo + "Your habits. Your rhythm." + App Store badge

### Video 2 — "AI That Gets You" (20 sec)
**Concept:** Showcase the AI coaching intelligence
- Open Coach tab — camera smoothly scrolls through insights
- Show: Pattern detection card "You skip Fridays 80% of the time"
- Show: Prediction card "Tomorrow: 78% chance of completing Exercise"
- Show: Health correlation "Workout success +40% with 7hr+ sleep"
- Transition: Weekly recap generating with key stats
- Show: AI model card "Trained on 45 data points. Accuracy: 82%"
- **End card:** "Intelligence that stays on your device." + Habitra logo

### Video 3 — "From 0 to Streak" (20 sec)
**Concept:** Show the streak-building journey compressed
- Day 1: Create first habit "Read 20 min" — tap +, name it, pick icon/color
- Day 3: Streak badge shows "3" — brief pulse
- Day 7: Milestone celebration — "1 Week!" with confetti
- Day 21: Stats view showing improving trend, A- health score
- Day 30: Share streak card — pick gradient theme, share to Messages
- Zoom out: Heatmap calendar fully green for the month
- **End card:** "Build habits that stick." + Habitra logo + App Store badge
